What is Pika

One creative studio, every model you need.

Pika began as an AI video generator built on its own proprietary model. In 2026 it rebuilt itself as a comprehensive AI creative platform — a set of focused apps for video, image, character and audio work that automatically route each job to the best model for the task, whether that's Pika 2.5, Seedance 2.5, MiniMax H3, Wan 3.0, GPT Image 2.5 or Nano Banana 2.

Alongside the studio, Pika ships its own frontier audio models (Music, Soundtrack, SFX and Speech), a developer-facing API Club with wholesale-level pricing on 100+ models, and an MCP toolkit that lets agents like Claude, Cursor and ChatGPT generate media directly. The classic Pika tools — Pikaframes, Pikaswaps, Pikadditions, Pikaffects and Pikaformance — still live inside the platform.

Available on web, iOS and Android, with a free tier and one-time credit packs so you never need a subscription to get started.

The Workflow

From idea to share, in four steps.

A simple loop that produces consistently better videos. Most creators tighten it into muscle memory after a few attempts.

Step 01

Build your ingredients first

Create the character in Character Studio and the product in Product Shot before touching Video Studio — then @mention them.

Step 02

Draft cheap, finish expensive

Iterate on Wan 3.0 (33 credits) or Pika Turbo, then commit the final take to Seedance 2.5 or Pika 2.5 at full resolution.

Step 03

Change one thing at a time

Lighting, camera, action, or style — adjust a single variable per generation to learn what moves the result.

Step 04

Grade, score, extend

Run Color Grade for mood, Pika Soundtrack for sound, and Extend Video if the cut needs breathing room. Save the prompt template.

Avoid The Pitfalls

Common mistakes, simple fixes.

Most "bad" Pika results trace back to a handful of predictable problems. Here is the fix list.

i

Scene looks messy or random

Reduce the number of objects, specify "clean background" or "minimal scene", and focus on one subject and one action per shot.

i

Character drifts between shots

Don't describe the character in text — build them once in Character Studio and @mention the same asset in every shot.

i

Burning through credits

Seedance 2.5 costs ~4× more per clip than Wan 3.0. Draft on the cheaper model, or buy a credit pack (60 credits per $1) rather than upgrading tiers.

i

Result doesn't match prompt

Add 1–2 specific cues like camera move and style. Remove conflicting style words. If auto-routing picked the wrong model, switch to manual selection.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What exactly is Pika in 2026? +
Pika is an AI creative platform combining video, image, character and audio generation in one studio. It runs its own models (Pika 2.5 for video; Pika Music, Soundtrack, SFX and Speech for audio) alongside partner models like Seedance 2.5, Wan 3.0, MiniMax H3, GPT Image 2.5 and Nano Banana 2, and picks the right one for each job automatically.
What changed in the September 2026 relaunch? +
The single prompt box became seven focused apps: Video Studio, Extend Video, Color Grade, Edit Image, Character Studio, Product Shot and Pika Soundtrack. Video Studio now produces multi-shot sequences up to 3 minutes with sound, and you can attach up to 50 characters, products, settings and styles and reference them with @mentions. Existing accounts, plans and creations carried over automatically.
Is Pika free to use? +
Yes. The free tier gives access to all models with no watermark and two parallel generations; instead of a monthly credit allowance you buy one-time credit packs at 60 credits per dollar. Paid plans start at $8/month (annual) for 900 credits, with Creator at $28/month adding a commercial license.
What is the Pika API Club? +
A $10/month developer membership (launched August 5, 2026) that unlocks wholesale-level, pay-as-you-go pricing on 100+ generative media models through one API — video (Seedance, Kling, Veo, Pika), image (GPT Image, Seedream, FLUX), audio (Pika Audio, MiniMax Music, ElevenLabs) and LLMs (Claude, Gemini). Pika claims up to 88% savings versus other aggregators. The first month includes $10 of usage credit.
What are the Pika Audio models? +
Four models released August 14–18, 2026: Pika Soundtrack (video-to-synchronized audio), Pika Music (full songs up to six minutes), Pika SFX (sound effects in under a second) and Pika Speech (voice cloning from five seconds of audio, 0.02 real-time factor). Pika describes them as the lowest-priced audio models on the market, up to 20× cheaper than comparable alternatives.
Can I use Pika videos for commercial projects? +
The Creator and Fancy subscriptions include a commercial license. Free and Starter tiers are intended for personal use. Always check the current Terms of Service inside your account, and remember third-party models routed through Pika carry their own usage terms.
How long can my videos be? +
Video Studio can produce multi-shot sequences up to about 3 minutes, sound included. Individual model limits still apply per shot — Pika 2.5 generates 5–10-second clips, Pikaframes runs up to 25 seconds, and Extend Video can lengthen any result.
What is Pikaformance? +
Pikaformance is Pika's talking-face model. Upload an image, drop in audio, and Pika animates lips, eyes, and expressions in sync with your sound. Pair it with Pika Speech to go from script to voice to face without recording anything.
Can Pika keep the same character across videos? +
This is what Character Studio is for. Build a character once, then @mention it in every shot of a Video Studio prompt — far more reliable than re-describing the character in text or reusing a single reference image.
Does Pika work with Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor? +
Yes. The Pika MCP (pika.me/mcp) lets agents call Pika's tools directly, and the API Club is built "agent-native" for Claude, Cursor, Codex and Lovable. The Founder Starter Kit ships four ready-made MCP skills: Build-a-Brand, App Store Screens, App Sizzle Video and Founder Video.
How does Pika compare to Runway and Sora? +
Pika is now a multi-model studio, so the comparison is less about one model. Runway still leads on fine-grained motion control for production work.
